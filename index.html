Our Story

About Us

Centuary Fab India Private Limited, established in July 2021, is the newest venture of the esteemed G.K. Jain Group, guided by our Chairman Mr. Ghevarchand Dalchand Jain (Mahaveer Chopra). Built upon decades of textile mastery, our company was founded with a singular mission — delivering world-class fabrics driven by innovation, integrity, and uncompromised quality.

Our Legacy

Our journey began in 1966 when Shree Ghewarchand Kesarimal Jain laid the foundation of the G.K. Jain brand as a premium fabric trading company. The business expanded into major textile hubs such as Ichalkaranji and Hubli before relocating operations to Erode — the Manchester of South India — where Dilip Kumar Mills marked a turning point in the group’s growth.

Carrying this legacy forward, Mahaveer Chopra alongside his brothers strengthened the G.K. Jain Group, establishing it as a respected force within the Indian textile industry.

A Legacy of Innovation

In 1995, Centuary Processing Mills was founded as a specialized processing unit offering bleaching, washing, and dyeing with cutting-edge machinery and advanced laboratories. With a production capacity of 100,000 metres per day, the company set new benchmarks in textile processing.

Today, the group enjoys a turnover exceeding 300+ million rupees and a nationwide network of 250+ dealers and agents, reinforcing its leadership in the domestic market.

A New Chapter Begins

To further strengthen his father’s vision, Mahaveer Chopra founded Centuary Fab India Private Limited. Backed by over 25 years of excellence from Centuary Processing Mills, the company guarantees exceptional fabric quality through modern technology, research-driven innovation, and a deeply customer-focused approach.
